# Vicente Gonzalez-Ruiz

## Biography

**Dr. Vicente González-Ruiz** is an Associate Professor in the
Informatics Department at the University of Almería. He earned his
M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Granada in 1992 and
his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Almería in
2000. He is a member of the university's [Supercomputing-Algorithms
Research Group](https://www.hpca.ual.es/).

With extensive expertise in image and video coding and transmission,
Dr. González-Ruiz has contributed to major open-source initiatives,
including the [P2PSP protocol](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/P2PSP),
Google Summer of Code, ESA Summer of Code in Space, and the
[JHelioviewer project](https://www.jhelioviewer.org/). His work in
signal processing focuses on interpolation and denoising algorithms
for microscopy, alongside software development for the [Calar Alto
Astronomical Observatory
(CAHA)](https://www.caha.es/es/). Additionally, he contributes to AI
applications that assist people with disabilities, such as fall
detection systems and enhanced user interaction within smart
environments.

As detailed in his [Curriculum
Vitae](https://vicente-gonzalez-ruiz.github.io/ridiculum/),
Dr. González-Ruiz has co-authored over 100 research papers and
participated in numerous research projects and contracts with private
companies and public institutions. He serves as the Principal
Investigator (PI) of the *Unidad de Supercomputación, Procesamiento de
Imagen y Nanotomografía* (USPIN), associated with the Spanish National
Research Council (CSIC). To date, he has supervised 5
Ph.D. dissertations and is currently advising 5 more.

Dr. González-Ruiz brings over 30 years of teaching experience to the
University of Almería.
