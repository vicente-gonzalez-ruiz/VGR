# Vicente Gonzalez-Ruiz

## Biography

**Dr. Vicente González-Ruiz** is an Associate Professor in the
Informatics Department at the University of Almería. He earned his
M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Granada in 1992 and
his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Almería in 2000. 

As a member of the [Supercomputing-Algorithms Research
Group](https://www.hpca.ual.es/), Dr. González-Ruiz has experience in
image/video coding and transmission, microscopy image/volume
processing, and IA systems for smart environments. As detailed in his
[Curriculum
Vitae](https://vicente-gonzalez-ruiz.github.io/ridiculum/), he has
co-authored over 100 research papers and participated in numerous
research projects and contracts with private companies and public
institutions. Currently, he serves as the Principal Investigator (PI)
of the *Unidad de Supercomputación, Procesamiento de Imagen y
Nanotomografía* (USPIN), associated with the Spanish National Research
Council (CSIC). He has contributed to major open-source initiatives,
including the [P2PSP protocol](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/P2PSP),
[Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/), [ESA
Summer of Code in
Space](https://www.esa.int/articles/socis-the-esa-summer-of-code-in-space),
and the [JHelioviewer project](https://www.jhelioviewer.org/). He has
collaborated with the [Calar Alto Astronomical Observatory
(CAHA)](https://www.caha.es/es/) in the development of software. He
has [supervised 5
Ph.D. dissertations](https://vicente-gonzalez-ruiz.github.io/ridiculum/#x1-2200011)
and is currently [advising 6
more](https://vicente-gonzalez-ruiz.github.io/ridiculum/#x1-2200011).

Dr. González-Ruiz brings over 30 years of [teaching
experience](https://vicente-gonzalez-ruiz.github.io/ridiculum/#x1-2300013)
to the University of Granada and University of Almería.
